For many mental health needs, group therapy can produce clinically comparable outcomes to individual therapy. So, why isn’t it used more? Dennis Morrison, PhD, chief clinical officer at Eleos, joined host Andra Hollenbeck to tackle that question on a recent episode of Jefferson Public Radio’s Mental Health Matters podcast.
