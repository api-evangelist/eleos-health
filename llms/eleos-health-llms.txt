# Eleos Health

> Eleos Health is the most widely adopted AI platform in behavioral and community-based healthcare. Its purpose-built workflow agents span clinical documentation, compliance, and revenue cycle management, embedding directly inside providers' existing web-based EHRs to reduce documentation time by ~70% and catch compliance risks and claim denials before submission. Founded in 2020 and headquartered in Waltham, Massachusetts.

Note: Eleos Health delivers its platform primarily through a lightweight browser-extension embedding into web-based EHRs; it does not publish a public developer portal or OpenAPI definition. ## Products

- Documentation — ambient in-session capture or quick text summaries turned into structured, payer-ready notes.
- Compliance — reviews every note against common risk areas (note cloning, empty notes, interventions, progress mentions, action plan, golden thread).
- Revenue Cycle Management — AI-powered eligibility checks and claim protection.
- Clinical Insights Agent — in-workflow clinical decision support.
